Source: Summary of public WHO guidance. Verify against the official documents before relying on it.

Physical activity: WHO recommends that adults get at least 150 to 300 minutes of moderate-intensity physical activity per week, plus muscle-strengthening activities on two or more days.

Diet: eat at least 400 grams (about five portions) of fruit and vegetables a day, choose whole grains, limit salt, and keep free sugars low, ideally under 10 percent of daily energy intake.

Sleep and stress: most adults need about 7 to 9 hours of sleep. Regular sleep, relaxation and social connection support heart and mental health.

Tobacco and alcohol: avoid all forms of tobacco, and limit alcohol. Both raise the risk of heart disease, cancer and liver problems.
