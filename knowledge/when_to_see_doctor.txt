Source: General safety guidance. This is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Seek urgent medical care immediately for chest pain or pressure, difficulty breathing, sudden weakness or numbness on one side of the body, trouble speaking, confusion, fainting, a severe sudden headache, or blood sugar or blood pressure that is extremely high with symptoms.

See a doctor soon if symptoms are persistent, getting worse, or unexplained, such as ongoing fever, unexplained weight loss, constant fatigue, or blood in urine or stool.

Do not self-medicate or change prescribed medicines without a doctor's advice. Share your medical reports and test results with a doctor so they can interpret them properly.

Regular health check-ups help detect problems such as high blood pressure, high blood sugar and high cholesterol early, even when you feel well.
